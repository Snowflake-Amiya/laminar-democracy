Tier-2 status

Open, repeatable criterion-referenced test; domain-scoped; status decays without re-certification.

Tier-3 status

Advanced certification in a technical domain; same openness and sunset rules as tier-2.

Decision tracks

Domain A - basic civic

Rights, values, ordinary legislation. Decided by the basic electorate.

Domain B - intermediate

Budgets, infrastructure, planning. Decided by the tier-2 electorate.

Domain C - highly technical

Monetary, nuclear, pharmaceutical. Decided by the tier-3 electorate.

Initiatives & safeguards

Tiered referendums

Basic: x signatures from all citizens.
Tier-2: x/y signatures from members.
Tier-3: x/z signatures from members.
Fixed window T; status holders sign basic petitions as ordinary signatories.

Democratic veto overlay

A general-electorate supermajority (55%) may overturn any tier decision within one electoral cycle.

Anti-capture: published item banks, adversarial review, random audits of the certification test.

Agenda access: any citizen may initiate at their own tier; thresholds fall with initiator status (x/y, x/z). Non-zero-sum status: criterion-referenced cutoffs let the certified share grow as citizens invest.
